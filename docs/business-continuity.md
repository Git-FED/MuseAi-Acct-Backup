# Business Continuity Plan

## Trigger conditions

Use this plan when an AI agent is unavailable, an account is inaccessible, an integration fails, a provider changes access behavior, or a credential may have been exposed.

## First 30 minutes

1. Record the time, affected agent, account, and observable symptoms.
2. Do not repeatedly retry a failing or locked workflow.
3. Check direct provider dashboards and human-held recovery channels.
4. Preserve evidence without copying secrets into tickets or chat.
5. Identify the manual fallback for each critical business process.

## Credential response

If a credential was displayed, pasted, exported, or may have been accessible to an unintended party, revoke it at the issuing provider and create a replacement. Do not wait for an AI agent to return it. Record rotation completion without recording the replacement value.

## Recovery tracker

Use an elapsed-time tracker from the first observed failure. Do not promise a recovery deadline unless the provider documents one. Record support case IDs and the next follow-up time.
